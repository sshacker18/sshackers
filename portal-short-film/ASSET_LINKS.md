# Asset Links

Use official library pages to find and download assets. Some libraries contain both free and paid items; check the exact asset's licence, attribution requirements, resolution, and alpha-channel format before using it in a released film.

## Portal / energy / VFX overlays
- ProductionCrate / FootageCrate: https://www.productioncrate.com/
  - Search: portal, energy ring, electricity, sparks, smoke, light rays.
  - Free-tier assets are available; some assets/features require a subscription.
- CreatorsFree: https://www.creatorsfree.in/
  - Search: sci-fi energy ring, shockwave, lightning.
  - Review the licence and file details on each download page.
- Mixkit stock video: https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/
  - Search: smoke, abstract, light, particles, space.
- Pexels videos: https://www.pexels.com/videos/
  - Search: blue particles, smoke, abstract energy, space.
- Pixabay videos: https://pixabay.com/videos/
  - Search: energy, particles, smoke, space.

## 3D environments, models, textures
- Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/
  - HDRIs, models and textures; assets are CC0 according to the library's terms.
- ambientCG: https://ambientcg.com/
  - Materials, textures and HDRIs; review the current licence information.
