# Shot Plan — Portal Opens in a Room

Target: 10–20 seconds; 24 fps; 16:9; 1080p or 4K.

## Shot 1 — The room (0–3 s)
- Static wide or medium-wide shot. Actor sits still, looking toward an empty area.
- Keep the camera locked on a tripod.
- Record 8–10 seconds of a clean plate with the actor out of frame.
- Capture room tone separately.

## Shot 2 — First sign (3–6 s)
- Actor notices a faint sound or light.
- Add a subtle blue flicker on one side of the actor/room.
- Add a low electrical hum and a few small particles.

## Shot 3 — Portal forms (6–10 s)
- Add the energy ring behind the actor, aligned to the room perspective.
- Animate scale/opacity from 0 to full over roughly 1–2 seconds.
- Add a short flash, sparks, and a whoosh.
- Mask the actor in front of the portal where needed.

## Shot 4 — Portal interior (10–14 s)
- Place a landscape/environment clip or 3D render inside the ring.
- Mask or corner-pin it to the portal plane.
- Add a soft edge glow and slight distortion at the boundary.

## Shot 5 — Reaction / reveal (14–20 s)
- Actor reaches toward the portal or slowly stands.
- Add blue light wrap to the actor, floor, and nearby objects.
- Finish with a bass impact, a brief camera shake, or a cut to black.

## On-set checklist
- Lock exposure, focus, and white balance where possible.
- Record the clean plate before moving the camera.
- Shoot a reaction take and a reach/interaction take.
- Keep the portal area clear enough for compositing.
